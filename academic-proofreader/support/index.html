What to include

Tell me what you were trying to do, what happened, and the plugin version if you know it. A short anonymised example is usually enough.

Please do not email full dissertations, confidential research, participant records, passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. Remove identifying details from screenshots.

Editing and citations

Check every proposed edit against your intended meaning and source material. If the plugin changes a technical term, citation or level of certainty, report the original passage and the suggested edit with unnecessary personal details removed.

Institutional questions

Your university or supervisor determines what assistance is allowed. Share the relevant policy with the plugin, and contact your institution where its wording is unclear.

Privacy and deletion

You can request deletion of support messages using the email above. Requests about ChatGPT conversation data or account settings must be directed to the host platform; I cannot delete those records from your account.

Support is provided by email without a guaranteed response time. Read the privacy notice for support-message handling.
