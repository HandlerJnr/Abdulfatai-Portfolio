Academic Proofreader

Your research. Your voice.

Careful academic proofreading for MSc, PGDip and doctoral students, and researchers across disciplines.

Created by Jamiu Abdulfatai, Academic Proofreader helps improve grammar, punctuation, clarity and flow while preserving your meaning, technical terminology, citations and level of certainty. British English is the default.

Small, useful edits

Receive a lightly edited version, a short explanation of significant changes, and questions or citation issues for your attention.

Attribution stays intact

Paraphrase suggestions address unclear wording or wording close to a supplied source. They retain attribution and never disguise copied material.

You approve the rewrite

Work section by section. Substantial rewrites are presented separately for your approval before they are applied.

How to begin

  1. Provide a section of research you wrote yourself.
  2. Add your assignment brief and institutional AI policy where available.
  3. Review the edits and resolve the questions before using the text.
Proofread this section in British English. Preserve my voice, citations and certainty. Make minimal edits and flag anything I should review.

Clear limits

The plugin does not invent findings or references, verify every fact, certify university compliance or guarantee any AI-detector score. If you supply a Turnitin report, it separates similarity matches from AI-writing indicators and treats indicators as prompts for review.
