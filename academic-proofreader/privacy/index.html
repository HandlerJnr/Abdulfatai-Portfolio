Academic Proofreader

Privacy, explained plainly.

This notice covers the current skills-only Academic Proofreader plugin and its information pages. Publisher: Jamiu Abdulfatai. Updated 7 October 2026.

Research in the host platform

The current plugin supplies editing instructions inside ChatGPT or a compatible host. It has no publisher-operated MCP server, external detector integration, research upload form, user database or payment system. Its package does not send research text to a publisher-operated service.

The host platform processes text and files you provide under its own terms, privacy policy and account settings. This is not local-only processing. Review OpenAI’s privacy policy when using ChatGPT. Other compatible hosts have their own policies. I cannot control or promise the host’s retention, training settings or deletion behaviour.

Support emails

If you contact aabdulfataijamiu@gmail.com, your email address, message, attachments and any name you provide are processed to answer your request, troubleshoot problems or handle a privacy request. Email is handled through Gmail and relevant email-delivery providers. Send only the minimum information needed.

Support correspondence is kept while a request is open and deleted within 90 days after resolution, except where a record must be retained to meet a legal obligation. You can request earlier deletion. Information is not collected through support for advertising or sold. Do not send confidential participant data or sensitive research material.

These web pages

The plugin information pages are hosted on Vercel. Loading a page involves normal hosting processing, which can include connection information such as IP address and technical request details. These pages contain no added analytics script, advertising tracker, research collection form or newsletter signup. Vercel operates the hosting infrastructure under its own policies; see Vercel’s privacy policy.

Requests and control

Contact the publisher at the email above about support information, including access, correction or deletion requests. For host-platform conversation records, use that platform’s privacy controls or contact it directly. Your applicable privacy rights depend on the processing and jurisdiction.

Changes and future payments

There is no paid subscription in this release. If accounts, payments, analytics or a publisher-operated backend are introduced, this notice will be updated to describe the providers, information handled, purposes and retention before that functionality is offered.
