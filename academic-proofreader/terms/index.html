Keep authorship in your hands.

These terms describe the current Academic Proofreader service provided by Jamiu Abdulfatai. Updated 7 October 2026.

Purpose and your work

Use the plugin to proofread your own academic writing, review clarity and attribution, and consider paraphrase suggestions. You retain ownership of your research. Providing text for editing does not transfer ownership to the publisher.

Your responsibility

Provide only material you are entitled to use. Review every edit, citation and claim before relying on it or submitting it. Follow your assignment requirements and institution’s rules about AI assistance, proofreading, research confidentiality and disclosure.

Do not use the plugin to fabricate findings, references or authorship evidence, conceal copied material, or evade AI or plagiarism detection. Substantial changes require your approval.

Limits of assistance

Outputs can contain errors. The plugin does not guarantee factual accuracy, citation completeness, detector scores, grades, publication acceptance or compliance with an institution’s rules. It is not an authoritative source for safety-critical, medical, legal or engineering decisions. Independent source checks remain necessary.
