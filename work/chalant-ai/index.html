Chalantwork: a live platform for sustainability data, evidence and review.

Sustainability reporting depends on more than entering figures. Teams need to connect the data to evidence, review it and use it across reporting and operational workflows.

Lead product designer · UX, interface design and design systems

I worked on product journeys, interface design and a shared design system for Chalant. The live sustainability platform brings reporting, emissions management, verification, capital access and safety operations together.

How the live platform is organised

  • Connect data to evidence and review. The live platform links entered data with supporting evidence and maker-checker approval before it is used for reporting and action.
  • Organise related sustainability work together. ESG reporting, emissions tracking, verification, finance and HSE operations are presented as connected workflows, with industry-specific routes.
  • Give teams a clear access route. The website supports enterprise access requests, with email and Microsoft sign-in for existing accounts.

What was delivered

Chalantwork is live, with email and Microsoft sign-in and enterprise access requests. Its published offering covers ESG reporting, emissions, verification, capital access and HSE operations.

The live website confirms product access and published scope. Usage figures and measured design outcomes are not published.
