Jamiu Abdulfatai is a product designer working across fintech, AI products and complex digital workflows.

He designs clear interfaces for web and mobile—from cross-border payments to banking across four subsidiaries. He’s currently studying MSc User Experience Design at the University of Brighton.

His work connects design with implementation: Figma, reusable design systems, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React. He designed this portfolio in Figma and brought it into production using AI-assisted coding.

He also holds a BSc (Hons) in Physics and a PGD in Aerospace Engineering from AFIT, Kaduna.
