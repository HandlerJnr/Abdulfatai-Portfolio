Making marketplace safety visible from checkout to payout.

Buyers need to trust independent sellers they may never meet. Sellers need the tools to fulfil those orders reliably.

End-to-end product designer · mobile app and website · 2026

I designed the buyer and seller journeys together: product discovery, store verification, checkout, escrow status and chat, alongside seller onboarding, KYC, inventory, orders and payouts.

The decisions behind the interface

  • Show who the buyer is dealing with. I placed verification at store level, where buyers assess the seller before paying.
  • Explain what happens to the payment. I designed escrow around visible stages, from protected payment through delivery confirmation to release of funds.
  • Connect shopping to fulfilment. Seller stock, orders and wallet tools follow the same transaction lifecycle as the buyer journey.

What was delivered

Horal is live on the web, iOS and Android. The released product includes verified stores, escrow, chat, seller inventory, order management and payouts.

Live releases confirm delivery. The published download count describes product adoption; it does not measure the effect of one design decision.
