01The short version

Horal is a live Nigerian marketplace for independent sellers. I owned product design across the mobile app and website. For buyers, that covered browsing, checkout, escrow, chat and order tracking. For sellers, it covered listing products, managing stock, fulfilling orders and receiving payouts.

02What I owned

I designed browsing, search, product pages, store pages, checkout, escrow status and chat. On the seller side, I covered onboarding and KYC, listings and stock variants, orders, store details, wallet and earnings. I also designed the responsive website.

03The challenge

Buyers are paying people they may not know. They need to see who the seller is, how the payment is protected and what happens after checkout. Sellers need enough detail to manage stock, delivery and payouts. I had to support both without filling the shopping screens with seller tools.

04The product model

Buyers find a product, check the seller, ask questions and pay with escrow protection. They can then follow the order. Sellers verify their identity, set up a store, list products and fulfil orders. Escrow connects the payment to delivery and the release of funds.

05Decision stories

01 / TRUST SIGNALS

Make verification part of the buying decision.

Problem
A buyer is deciding whether to trust a seller they may never have met.
Constraint
Show the safety checks without making shopping feel like a series of warnings.
Decision
I made store verification visible to buyers so they could use it when choosing a seller.
Evidence
Store verification and KYC are shipping capabilities documented in the live app listings.
02 / ESCROW

Turn escrow into a journey, not a badge.

Problem
A payment badge does not tell either side what is happening to the money after checkout.
Constraint
Both sides need to understand the payment status as the seller fulfils the order.
Decision
I designed states for protected payment, order progress, confirmed delivery and released funds.
Evidence
Google Play documents escrow-protected transactions in the live product.
03 / COMMUNICATION

Keep questions inside the protected transaction.

Problem
Product and fulfilment questions are inevitable before and after payment.
Constraint
Taking the conversation to another app separates it from the listing and order.
Decision
I included chat in the buying and selling journey so questions stay with the transaction.
Evidence
In-app chat with image sharing is included in the shipping product notes.
04 / SELLER OPERATIONS

Design the marketplace from both sides.

Problem
Buyer trust collapses if sellers cannot reliably manage inventory, orders and payouts.
Constraint
Operational depth must not turn the shopping experience into an admin tool.
Decision
I connected stock variants, orders, store details, wallet and earnings in the seller tools.
Evidence
These seller capabilities are documented in the current public app-store release notes.

06What shipped

The live product supports both buyers and sellers. Public app-store notes list stock variants, seller wallet and earnings, orders, store details and location, KYC, verification and chat with image sharing. Google Play also documents verified vendors, escrow protection and Paystack-powered Naira payments.

07Live proof

Horal is live on the web, iOS and Android. Public store listings document the buyer and seller features described here. These listings show what is available; they do not establish a measured effect on buyer trust or conversion.
