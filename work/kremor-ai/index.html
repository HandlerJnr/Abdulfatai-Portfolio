Working demo

Helping people turn a clothing idea into a design that can move toward production.

Someone may know the occasion, textile or feeling they want without knowing how to prompt an AI tool. The generated idea also needs a route into the storefront and garment-production workflow.

UI/UX Designer - Design & Research Lead · Kremor AI · June 2024–July 2026

I led design across the generative workspace, storefront and role-based operations, using one shared system. I also worked on model benchmarking, annotation and qualitative evaluation across complex AI interaction tasks.

The decisions behind the interface

  • Offer a starting point before asking for a prompt. Quick actions and conversational examples help people describe an idea without specialist design vocabulary.
  • Name the textiles people are working with. The interface uses Ankara, Aso-Oke, Adire, Kente and Afro-fusion instead of a broad label that loses useful detail.
  • Connect creative work to operational roles. The design covers the handoff from an approved idea toward production. The five-role workshop permissions model is marked for validation with operating teams.

What was delivered

Three connected product surfaces share one design system. The founder’s reference confirms my combined UI/UX design and AI-evaluation work.

This evaluation work concerned AI outputs, rather than participant usability sessions. The workshop role and permissions model still needs validation.
