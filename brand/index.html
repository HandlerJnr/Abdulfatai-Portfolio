Let’s talk

I design clear interfaces for payments, banking and complex workflows.

I work across web and mobile, from cross-border payments to banking across four subsidiaries. I’m studying MSc User Experience Design at the University of Brighton.

I’m usually drawn to the part of a product where things get complicated. That’s where I find the most interesting design problems.

More about me +

[The person behind the work]

I like untangling the things that make digital products difficult to use. Sometimes that means questioning a business rule; sometimes it means finding a simpler way to explain what’s happening.

I enjoy the design process, but I also like understanding how things are built. It helps me make decisions that work beyond the Figma file, and gives me more useful conversations with engineers.

At Brighton, I’m developing my research practice and spending more time questioning the decisions behind interfaces, especially how research and AI can help us design more responsibly. My AI practice is part of that exploration.

When I’m not thinking about interfaces, there’s a good chance I’m somewhere playing football.
