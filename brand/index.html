[Hi there]

I’m Jamiu, a product designer who enjoys figuring out why something feels harder than it should. I design for fintech and complex digital workflows, especially when money, trust or someone else’s approval changes what happens next. From escrow-backed shopping to international education payments and multi-market banking, I turn complicated requirements into clearer journeys.

I like understanding the rules and edge casesrules and edge cases before getting attached to a screenscreen. I work closely with founders, product and engineering teams because a decision is only useful when the people building the product understand why it was made.

I’m studying MSc User Experience Design at the University of Brighton, strengthening my research practice and exploring responsible uses of AIresponsible uses of AI alongside design systemsdesign systems and front-end prototypes. Read about my AI practice and how it supports my work.
