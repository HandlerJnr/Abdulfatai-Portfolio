HOW I WORK

Good design starts before the interface.

I work from problem framing through research, flows, interface design, prototyping, testing and developer handoff — keeping user needs and business outcomes visible throughout.

01

Understand the problem

Stakeholder goals, user needs, constraints, competitors and the real friction behind the request.

02

Structure the experience

Flows, information architecture and low-fidelity thinking before visual polish.

03

Design and prototype

High-fidelity responsive interfaces with reusable components, accessibility and interaction states.

04

Test, refine and ship

Feedback loops with users, stakeholders and engineers to reduce uncertainty before release.
