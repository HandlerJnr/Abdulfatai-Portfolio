A BIOGRAPHY OF ME

I’m Jamiu Abdulfatai, a UI designer, UX researcher and product designer from Nigeria, currently based in the UK. I design digital products for teams that care about clarity, craft and experiences that actually work.

I’ve worked across SaaS, marketplaces, healthcare, fintech and consumer products—turning complicated problems into simple, thoughtful experiences people can actually use. I care about the details, but I care just as much about what happens after the pixels leave Figma: whether the product ships, solves the right problem and creates real value for the business behind it.

Some of my work has involved building products from the ground up, designing complex systems, shaping product strategy and working closely with teams to take ideas from messy beginnings to something people can use.
