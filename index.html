HOW I WORK

From problem to shipped.

The process changes with the product, but the principle stays the same: reduce uncertainty before polishing the interface.

01

Frame the real problem

Before anything is drawn: what is actually going wrong, for whom, and why it matters.

02

Map roles & constraints

Users, permissions, business rules, regulators, edge cases and the system around the screen.

03

Explore directions

More than one answer held against the same product goal rather than defended on taste.

04

Prototype the risk

Build the part most likely to be wrong first — not the part easiest to make beautiful.

05

Test clarity

Can people understand the flow, predict what happens next and recover when something fails?

06

Build the system

Components, variants, tokens and patterns so the tenth screen costs less than the first.

07

Ship with engineering

Specs, front-end review and decisions that survive implementation instead of stopping at handoff.

08

Measure what changed

Separate design impact from company traction and keep the evidence honest.

RADIUS · 2025

Cross-Border Payments

RoleProduct DesignerPlatformMobile app + marketing siteStatusLive product

The problem

International education payments are deadlines, not ordinary transactions. The payer is often a parent or relative, while the beneficiary is the student — breaking the model most payment apps assume.

Key decisions

I made sponsored payment a first-class transfer type, surfaced verification and currency as everyday context, used the immigration journey’s own vocabulary and moved credibility work into the marketing site before the app install.

Product context

Radius reports 100K+ international students, 1,500+ five-star reviews, 96+ countries served and a six-hour average processing time. Those are company-reported product metrics, not claims of design impact.

AI Learning Platform & Agent Workspace

RoleLead Product DesignerPlatformResponsive webStatusPrototype

The problem

The skill people need around AI is not only prompting — it is judgement: what to delegate, when to trust the output and when a confident answer should be rejected.

Connected Healthcare & Telemedicine

RoleEnd-to-End Product DesignerScope7-step care journeySurfacesPatient · Provider · HMO · Pharmacy
