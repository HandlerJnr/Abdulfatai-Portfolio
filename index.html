A BIOGRAPHY OF ME

I’m Jamiu Abdulfatai, a product designer based in Brighton, UK. I turn complex fintech, SaaS and AI workflows into clear, usable experiences, working from requirements and early flows through design systems and developer handoff.

I work close to founders, product and engineering: mapping journeys, writing requirements, building reusable systems and reviewing implementation so the reasoning survives handoff rather than stopping at the Figma file.

Across Bizinc, Vista and Kremor AI, that has meant progressing into design leadership, supporting four localised banking subsidiaries, and connecting AI creation to real production workflows.
