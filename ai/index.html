HOW I THINK ABOUT AI

The useful part is usually outside the model.

Good AI product work is often about restraint: what not to automate, what to show, where to ask for confirmation, and when the system should stop pretending it knows.

01

Evidence before fluency.

A polished answer is not proof. Keep source evidence separate from model confidence.

02

Review before release.

Make responsibility visible through approvals, permissions and clear ownership.

03

Use AI where it earns its place.

Sometimes the right interaction is a recommendation, a review queue or one suggested next step — not another chatbot.

THE STANDARD

AI can accelerate the work.
It does not get to invent the evidence.
