04 / OPERATING PRINCIPLES

The intelligence can move fast. The product still needs judgement.

These are the constraints I keep around AI work so usefulness does not outrun evidence, responsibility or user control.

01

Evidence before fluency.

A polished answer is not proof. Keep the source visible and separate what is known from what is inferred.

02

Human review stays visible.

Confidence, approval states and permissions should make responsibility clear instead of hiding it behind automation.

03

Useful output over AI theatre.

The best AI interaction may be a workflow, a review queue or a single recommended next step — not another chat interface.

MY STANDARD

AI can accelerate the work. It does not get to invent the evidence.
