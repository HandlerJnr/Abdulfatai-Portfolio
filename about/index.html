About Jamiu.

I’m Jamiu Abdulfatai, a product designer based in Brighton, UK. I work on fintech, SaaS, marketplace and AI products, from the first requirements to the designs developers build.

I map journeys, write requirements and build reusable components with founders, product teams and developers. I also review the build, because the details need to work in the finished product.

How I use AI in research, design and delivery ↗

At Horal, I owned mobile and web product design. At Radius, I designed payment journeys for students and families. At Bizinc, I progressed into design leadership. At Vista, I worked on banking across four markets.
